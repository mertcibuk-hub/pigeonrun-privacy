Privacy Policy

Pigeon Run · Last updated: 24 October 2026

Pigeon Run is a flying game. It does not ask you to create an account or register, and we do not collect personal information about you.

Stored on your device

A small amount of game data is kept only in your device's local storage: whether you have bought "Remove Ads" and how many runs you have played. None of it is sent to our servers; it is removed when you delete the app.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad that lets you keep flying after a hawk catches you, and occasional ads between runs. AdMob may collect information such as your advertising identifier, device model and OS version, coarse location (country/region) and ad interactions in order to select and measure ads. We do not collect or see this data; it is processed directly by Google: Google Privacy & Terms.

On first launch the game asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency); in the EEA and UK Google's consent form is also shown. If you decline, the game works fully and ads are simply not personalised. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In-app purchases

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

The game is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from users under 13.

Data sales and changes

We do not sell or rent any data. If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
